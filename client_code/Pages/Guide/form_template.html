How to trace a wall

From a picture on your phone to pencil lines on your wall, in about ten minutes of setup.

1What you need

A Meta Quest

Quest 3 or 3S (colour passthrough) is ideal. Quest Pro and Quest 2 work too, in black and white.

A soft pencil

HB or 2B, sharpened. Chalk or a chalk pen for dark walls. An eraser that doesn't smudge.

A picture

Anything: your drawing, a photo, a logo, or one from the library. Prepare it on any screen.

Good light

Passthrough needs a lit room. Daylight or a bright lamp, no light shining straight at the headset.

Optional extras

Painter's tape, a long ruler or a level for straight lines, a step stool for high walls.

Or an Android phone

Phones with Google Play Services for AR work too. Use a tripod so your hands are free.

2Set up the headset

Run Space Setup (Quest 3 / 3S)

Settings → Physical space → Space setup. Let it scan the walls. WallTracer then finds the wall exactly where you point, with no guessing.

Draw a boundary that includes the wall

You'll stand right next to it. If the boundary is in the way, redraw it a little past the wall, or use a stationary boundary near it.

Open WallTracer in the Meta Quest Browser

Go to this site, sign in with the same account, open the Studio and press Trace, then Start on the wall. Allow the browser to use your space and hands when it asks.

Turn on hand tracking

Settings → Movement tracking → Hand and body tracking: on, and auto-switch from controllers to hands: on. Then the moment you put the controllers down, your hands take over.

3Trace a wall

Prepare the picture

In the studio, add a picture and choose a look: Lines for drawings, Outline for photos, Tones for murals you'll paint in. Set its real width. A grid and corner crosses help a lot for big walls.

Place it

  • Aim at the wall: a ring shows where the wall is. Pull the trigger.
  • Touch 3 points: touch the wall with the controller tip at the centre, then two more spots a hand-width apart. Works without Space Setup, and is very precise.
  • Float in front: it hangs in the air; move it with the grip.

Adjust

Wrong spot? Just point somewhere else on the wall and pull the trigger again: it jumps there. Hold the grip and move your hand to drag it, or use the left stick for small slides. Right stick up and down for size, left and right to turn it. The menu has Level, Push in and Pull out (2 mm steps).

Looks a little behind or in front of the wall when you move your head? Touch the real wall with a controller tip next to the picture and pull the trigger: the picture snaps to that exact depth, and WallTracer remembers the fix for your next placements.

Lock it

Click the right stick (or press Lock in place in the menu). It's pinned to the wall now: put the controllers down and pick up your pencil. When you're done, hold Y for two seconds to leave VR and land back on the website.

Trace, and check

Trace the lines. Pinch with your free hand (or press A / X) to hide the picture and see what you've drawn; pinch again to bring it back. Blink does it for you every few seconds.

One square at a time

With the grid on, Spotlight shows one square and dims the rest. Mark it done, move to the next. Your progress is saved.

Come back tomorrow

Trace the corner crosses first. Next time, the headset may remember the spot by itself; if not, place it again and line the crosses up. Your size, look and finished squares are kept.

6Tips for great results

Neon on light walls

Cyan and magenta read best through passthrough. Keep "dark edge" on for pale walls; white lines for dark walls.

Lower the opacity

At 35 to 60% you see your pencil through the picture, so you know which lines are done.

Lines, not shading

Trace outlines and the borders between tones. Paint the shading in afterwards with the picture beside you.

Big walls: squares

Use the grid and Spotlight. Finish a square, mark it, move on. Great for sharing the work with a friend.

Your hand is not in the way

The picture draws over your hand. That's normal: look at your pencil tip, or lower the opacity.

Straight lines

For geometric pieces, mark the ends of each line, then join them with a ruler with the headset off.

7Troubleshooting

"Start on the wall" isn't there

Your browser can't show passthrough. On a Quest, use the Meta Quest Browser (not a third-party one) and make sure it's up to date. On a computer you can still practise in the virtual room.

The picture seems to float behind or in front of the wall

The headset's room scan can be a few centimetres off. While lining up, touch the real wall with a controller tip and pull the trigger to snap the picture to it. The fix is remembered. Re-running Space Setup can also help.

The ring doesn't appear on my wall

The headset doesn't know your walls yet. Run Space Setup, or choose Touch 3 points in the menu: it works anywhere.

The picture drifts or jumps

Tracking needs light and some texture. Turn on more lights; a completely blank white wall is hard. If it moved, unlock it and line it up with your crosses again.

It hid when I didn't want it to

Pinches with your pencil hand are ignored, but check which hand is set as your pencil hand in the project's settings.

It's the wrong size

Set the width in the studio, or use the right stick before locking. Measure the wall with a tape for a perfect fit.

Can I trace on paper or a table?

Yes: aim at a table or the floor. The top of the picture points away from you.
