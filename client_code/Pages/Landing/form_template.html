How it works

From a picture on your phone to pencil lines on your wall.

Pick a picture

Your drawing, a photo, a logo, lettering, or a stencil from the library. Choose its look and real size.

Pin it to the wall

In the headset, aim at the wall and pull the trigger, or touch the wall at three points. Slide, resize, lock.

Put the controllers down

The picture stays exactly where you locked it. Pick up your pencil and trace, hands free.

Hide it to check

Press A or X, or pinch your free hand: the picture vanishes and you see only your lines. Pinch again to carry on.

Better than a projector

The old trick for murals, minus the dark room and the shadows.

A projector

  • Needs a dark room
  • Keystone distortion on anything but a square-on wall
  • Your body casts a shadow on the picture
  • Cables, stands and a projector to buy

WallTracer

  • Works in daylight, in passthrough
  • No distortion, at any angle, on walls, tables and floors
  • Nothing to block: the picture is in your eyes
  • Just the headset you already own

Questions

Which headsets work?

Meta Quest 3 and 3S give the best, full-colour passthrough. Quest Pro and Quest 2 work too (Quest 2 in black and white). Android phones with AR work on a tripod.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open this site in the Meta Quest Browser, sign in and press Trace. Prepare your pictures on any computer or phone first.

How accurate is it?

Very, once it's locked: within a few millimetres on a well-lit wall. For big murals, trace the corner crosses first so you can line it up again any time.

Will the picture stay still when I put the controllers down?

Yes. Locking pins it to the room (with a spatial anchor when the headset supports it), and hand tracking takes over.

Is it free?

Yes, WallTracer is free. Without an account you can try everything; with one, your projects are saved and waiting in your headset.

Who can see my pictures?

Only you. Projects are private. Only photos you choose to share in the Showcase are public, after a moderator approves them.
